## 🔬 Research

### 🌾 AgriML-BD: Crop Yield Prediction & Recommendation
*Ongoing · Submitted Jun 2026 · Supervised by Ms. Sadia Islam, Asst. Professor, Dept. of CSE, UIU*

An ML system that combines **crop recommendation** and **yield prediction** in a Flask web app, comparing Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, and DNN models.

| Best R² (Regression) | Best Accuracy | Experiments | Dataset |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **0.9455** | **94.03%** | **18** | 4,607 rows |

**My role: Data Engineer.** I led data collection, integration, and preprocessing.
